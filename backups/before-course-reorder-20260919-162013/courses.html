IN PROGRESS CPRE 3810

Computer Organization and Assembly Level Programming

Course overview

Introduction to Computer Architecture and Organization, instruction set design and assembly language programming, control flow instruction, procedure calls, stack management, datapath and control, processor design, memory and I/O systems

IN PROGRESS CPRE 3300

Integrated Systems

Course overview

• Introduction to semiconductor technology, integrated circuit design, and digital systems.
• Modeling of IC devices including BJTs, MOSFETs, Diodes, Thyristors etc.
• Analysis of both digital and analog building blocks

What I'm doing

• Learn semiconductor physics, device modeling, and IC fabrication.
• Explore digital and analog circuit design, including logic gates, flip-flops, amplifiers, and filters.
• Learn physical layout, circuit simulation, digital building blocks, and digital synthesis.
• Analysis and design of analog building blocks.
• Design and implement a complete integrated system using both digital and analog components by end of semester.

Tools / Skills

Semiconductor PhysicsDevice ModelingIC FabricationDigital Component DesignAnalog Component Design
