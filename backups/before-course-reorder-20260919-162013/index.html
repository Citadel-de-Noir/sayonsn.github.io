About me

I'm a student at Iowa State University double majoring in Computer Engineering and Electrical Engineering who enjoys working across the boundary between code and physical systems. I'm especially interested in VLSI, Semiconductor Design, Computer Architecture, Audio-Visual Systems, and Power Electronics.

I like projects where the product is a not just a solution to a problem, but also a discovery that has soul and a personal connection, that combines both technical and creative elements, and sometimes just a silly "What If" given shape.
