Underwater
Mine Seeker.

Manual control, autonomous decisions, and a scanning sensor head.

A team robotics project using a ground-based CyBot and the TI TM4C123GH6PM microcontroller to simulate a naval mine-search mission.

Embedded CTM4C123GH6PMRobot control
GitHub · Coming soon
S

Situation

Searching a simulated minefield

Our CPRE 288 team built a robot to explore a course containing obstacles and simulated mine targets. It needed to support both operator control and autonomous navigation.

T

Task

Connect sensing to behavior

I was responsible for the main program, movement routines, and behavior logic in both operating modes. My work also covered the timer, scan, ultrasonic ping, and servo modules.

A

Action

Coordinate movement and scanning

I implemented manual driving and autonomous decisions for scanning, investigating targets, choosing gaps, and recovering from bump or cliff detections. I integrated servo positioning, ultrasonic ranging, and timing with the main control loop, including stop requests and manual takeover. These pieces worked alongside teammates’ modules and course-provided utilities.

R

Result

A combined control and sensing implementation

The final firmware brought manual and autonomous behavior together with the scanning system. A saved scan reported a target at 121°, about 25.75 cm away, with an estimated width of 4.51 cm—concrete evidence of the sensing pipeline producing target data.
