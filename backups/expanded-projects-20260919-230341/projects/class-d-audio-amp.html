S

Situation

Explore another amplifier architecture

This club project introduced the TPA3122 Class-D design alongside the analog amplifier builds. Its switching output stage brings filtering and power-supply components into sharper focus.

T

Task

Build the TPA3122 amplifier board

I assembled the club’s amplifier design under Dr. Gary Tuttle’s guidance. The task involved identifying a larger set of capacitors and understanding how the power, control, and output sections fit together.

A

Action

Assemble the filters and controls

The build starts with resistors and small capacitors, followed by connectors, the IC socket, output inductors, and control headers. Bootstrap and bypass capacitors support the chip, while the output filter and coupling components connect it to the speakers. Jumpers select gain and control mute and shutdown.

R

Result

Add switching-amplifier experience

I completed the Class-D amplifier project as part of my club work. The build expanded my experience with component placement, output filtering, and hardware configuration across different audio amplifier designs.
